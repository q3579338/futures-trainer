Perpetual futures · simulator

Practice perpetual futures with real prices and fake money

A USDⓈ-M perpetual futures simulator: live market data, simulated funds, exchange-style order entry. Feel leverage, funding and liquidation before you risk a single real dollar.

Open the simulator →

The simulator is available in English and Chinese (switch languages from the ⋯ menu). Order forms follow the layout of a major exchange, so the workflow is familiar right away.

Real market data

Prices, order-book depth and funding rates stream from the real market; only the money is simulated.

Exchange-grade orders

Isolated or cross margin, limit and market orders, conditional orders, trailing stops, take-profit and stop-loss.

Liquidation by the exchange formula

The liquidation price uses the exchange's maintenance-margin brackets, so a wipe-out here looks exactly like a real one.

Trade behaviour analysis

Every fill is recorded: PnL by symbol, win rate, holding time, chasing and revenge-trading patterns.

What it is for

  • Learning how leverage, margin and funding actually move a position.
  • Testing a strategy against live prices without paying tuition to the market.
  • Rehearsing the discipline that a real risk desk expects: sizing, stops, cooling off after a loss.
No real funds. Nothing on this site touches an exchange account, a wallet or a payment method. Balances are simulated and can be reset at any time.

Part of the satloot family

The simulator is one of a set of small, open tools built by one person. The risk desk at earn.satloot.com scans the market and enforces trading discipline; the block-hash universes at bnbbang.com and bang.satloot.com turn BNB and Bitcoin blocks into simulated cosmoses.
